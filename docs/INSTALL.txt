NotchLyrics 0.1.0-beta.1

Requires macOS 13 or newer and the Spotify desktop app.
Universal binary: Apple Silicon and Intel. Runtime tested on Apple Silicon only.

1. Drag NotchLyrics.app onto Applications.
2. Eject this disk image and open NotchLyrics from Applications.
3. This personal beta is ad-hoc signed and NOT notarized by Apple. macOS may block
   the first launch. If you trust this release, try opening the app once, then
   use System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway and confirm.
   Managed Macs may prohibit this. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally.
4. Open Spotify desktop and play a track. Allow Automation access to Spotify
   when macOS asks. You can review it in Privacy & Security > Automation.
5. Hover the top-center notch to open the player. Settings opens lyric timing
   and the Always show lyric toggle. Positive timing advances lyrics.
6. Quit from the music-note menu bar item > Keluar, or right-click the notch.

No Dock icon is expected: NotchLyrics runs as a menu bar app.
Lyrics are fetched from LRCLIB; availability and timing vary by track/version.
Title, artist, album and duration are sent to LRCLIB. Artwork loads from the URL
provided by Spotify. No analytics, accounts or listening history are stored.

Releases: https://github.com/denjayx/NotchLyrics/releases
Issues: https://github.com/denjayx/NotchLyrics/issues

For older prototype installations: quit the old app before replacing it.
If Automation fails after replacing it, review its permission in System Settings.
